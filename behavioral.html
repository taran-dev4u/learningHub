STAR Framework & Storytelling

3 subsections · 12 concepts
0 / 12
Every behavioral answer needs structure.
When. Every behavioral question. Period.
Key idea. Situation (10%) → Task (10%) → Action (60%) → Result (20%). Spend most time on YOUR actions, not context.

1.1

STAR Components

4 concepts
What each letter means and how much time it deserves.
  1. ☆
    S — Situation (10%)
    Brief context. What was happening? Who? When? Keep to 2-3 sentences.
    🔍YT🔍G
  2. ☆
    T — Task (10%)
    What YOU specifically were responsible for. Not the team — you.
    🔍YT🔍G
  3. ☆
    A — Action (60%)
    Specific actions YOU took, in order. Use 'I' not 'we'. Include thought process and trade-offs.
    🔍YT🔍G
  4. ☆
    R — Result (20%)
    Quantify the outcome. Metrics > adjectives. Include what you learned.
    🔍YT🔍G
1.2

Common Mistakes

5 concepts
How candidates lose points.
  1. ☆
    Saying 'we' instead of 'I'
    Interviewer is evaluating YOU. Be specific about your individual contribution.
    🔍YT🔍G
  2. ☆
    Too much context, no action
    If you spend 3 minutes on Situation, you've already lost the room.
    🔍YT🔍G
  3. ☆
    Vague results
    'Made things better' ≠ 'reduced p99 latency 40% saving $200k/year'.
    🔍YT🔍G
  4. ☆
    No reflection / learning
    Senior signal: what would you do differently? What did you learn?
    🔍YT🔍G
  5. ☆
    Choosing the wrong story
    Match story to question. A 'conflict' story for a 'leadership' question hurts you.
    🔍YT🔍G
1.3

Variants of STAR

3 concepts
When STAR isn't enough.
  1. ☆
    SOAR (Situation, Obstacle, Action, Result)
    Explicitly call out the obstacle. Good when challenges are the point.
    🔍YT🔍G
  2. ☆
    CAR (Context, Action, Result)
    Shorter form. Good for follow-up questions or time-pressed rounds.
    🔍YT🔍G
  3. ☆
    Amazon's STAR + Learning
    Always add 'what I'd do differently'. Amazon weights self-reflection heavily.
    🔍YT🔍G

Story preparation checklist

6 concepts
How to write a great story.
  1. ☆
    Pick a story with quantified impact
    Numbers > adjectives. % improved, $ saved, users affected.
    🔍YT🔍G
  2. ☆
    Time-box: under 3 minutes spoken
    Practice with a stopwatch. 4+ minutes = interviewer disengages.
    🔍YT🔍G
  3. ☆
    Include trade-offs and alternatives considered
    Shows judgment. Senior signal.
    🔍YT🔍G
  4. ☆
    End with reflection / what you'd do differently
    Self-awareness without self-flagellation.
    🔍YT🔍G
  5. ☆
    Practice out loud, not in your head
    Saying it sounds nothing like thinking it. Record yourself.
    🔍YT🔍G
  6. ☆
    Map every story to a question category
    Build a 12-story × 8-category matrix.
    🔍YT🔍G

5.1

Before the interview

3 concepts
1-2 weeks out.
  1. ☆
    Research the company's values + recent news
    Show you've done homework. Mention specific products or values.
    🔍YT🔍G
  2. ☆
    Map your stories to their values
    If interviewing at Amazon: which LPs does each story hit? Same for any company's values.
    🔍YT🔍G
  3. ☆
    Prepare questions to ask interviewer
    5-7 thoughtful questions per round. Asking nothing = red flag.
    🔍YT🔍G
5.2

During the interview

5 concepts
In the moment.
  1. ☆
    Listen for which LP / value they're probing
    Question phrasing tells you. 'Customer' → Customer Obsession. 'Disagreed' → Have Backbone.
    🔍YT🔍G
  2. ☆
    Use 'I' not 'we'
    Be specific about YOUR contribution.
    🔍YT🔍G
  3. ☆
    Quantify everything you can
    Numbers, percentages, dollar amounts, time saved.
    🔍YT🔍G
  4. ☆
    Pause when needed
    Better to think for 5 seconds than ramble for 30. Silence is fine.
    🔍YT🔍G
  5. ☆
    Take notes during your own answer
    Helps you stay structured (S→T→A→R) and reference back.
    🔍YT🔍G

Common pitfalls to avoid

5 concepts
Easy mistakes that cost offers.
  1. ☆
    Memorized-sounding answers
    Practice the points, not the script. Memorized = robotic.
    🔍YT🔍G
  2. ☆
    Bashing previous employer/coworkers
    Even if justified, it makes YOU look bad. Stay neutral or positive.
    🔍YT🔍G
  3. ☆
    Vague 'I think I'm good at...' claims
    Show, don't tell. Replace claims with stories.
    🔍YT🔍G
  4. ☆
    Not knowing the company
    Basic homework. Mission, recent products, recent news, key competitors.
    🔍YT🔍G
  5. ☆
    Asking only about salary/perks
    Save compensation for after the offer. Ask about role, team, growth.
