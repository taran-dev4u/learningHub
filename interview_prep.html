Behavioral Story Methods

5 items
How to turn experience into concise stories.
  1. ☆
    STAR method
    Situation, Task, Action, Result. Best default for behavioral questions.
    YTGoogle
  2. ☆
    CARL method
    Context, Action, Result, Learning. Strong when reflection matters.
    YTGoogle
  3. ☆
    SOAR method
    Situation, Obstacle, Action, Result. Useful for conflict, blockers, and ambiguity.
    YTGoogle
  4. ☆
    PAR method
    Problem, Action, Result. Fast format for shorter recruiter screens.
    YTGoogle
  5. ☆
    Result plus reflection
    End with what changed, what you learned, and how you apply it now.
    YTGoogle

Direct Answer Methods

5 items
How to answer open-ended and opinion questions without rambling.
  1. ☆
    PREP method
    Point, Reason, Example, Point. Good for opinions and trade-off questions.
    YTGoogle
  2. ☆
    PEEL method
    Point, Evidence, Explanation, Link. Good for leadership and judgment answers.
    YTGoogle
  3. ☆
    Headline first
    Start with the answer in one sentence before giving context.
    YTGoogle
  4. ☆
    Signposting
    Tell the interviewer the two or three parts of your answer before diving in.
    YTGoogle
  5. ☆
    Time-boxing
    Prepare 30-second, 90-second, and 3-minute versions of core stories.
    YTGoogle

Trade-Off and Judgment Questions

5 items
Show senior reasoning in simple language.
  1. ☆
    Why did you choose this technology
    Name alternatives and the constraint that mattered most.
    YTGoogle
  2. ☆
    What did you optimize for
    State the ranking: reliability, latency, cost, speed, or simplicity.
    YTGoogle
  3. ☆
    What broke at scale
    Explain bottleneck, symptom, mitigation, and long-term fix.
    YTGoogle
  4. ☆
    How do you handle incidents
    Detect, mitigate, communicate, root-cause, prevent.
    YTGoogle
  5. ☆
    How do you decide build vs buy
    Compare cost, control, speed, risk, and team expertise.
    YTGoogle

Coding Interview Communication

5 items
What to say while solving.
  1. ☆
    Restate the problem
    Confirm inputs, outputs, constraints, and examples.
    YTGoogle
  2. ☆
    Clarify edge cases
    Ask about nulls, empty data, duplicates, size, and ordering.
    YTGoogle
  3. ☆
    Explain brute force first
    Show baseline understanding before optimizing.
    YTGoogle
  4. ☆
    Narrate the invariant
    Explain what stays true during the algorithm.
    YTGoogle
  5. ☆
    Close with complexity and tests
    Give time, space, and targeted test cases.
