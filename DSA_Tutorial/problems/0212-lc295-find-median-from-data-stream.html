📋 The problem

Design a structure that accepts a stream of numbers and can return the median of everything seen so far at any time.

Restated in our own words — the LeetCode icon above opens the formal statement with exact constraints and examples.

Where this fits

Two Heaps for Median Finding — Max-heap for the lower half, min-heap for the upper half — top of either gives the median.

💡 Key idea
Two heaps: max-heap lower half, min-heap upper half, rebalanced so sizes differ ≤ 1; median from the tops.

Why this pattern?

  • "k largest / k closest / k most frequent / k-th …"
  • merge k sorted lists/streams
  • "median of a stream" / balance two halves → two heaps
  • scheduling: always take the earliest end / most frequent / cheapest next

import heapq
# Top-k largest: min-heap of size k
h = []
for x in nums:
    heapq.heappush(h, x)
    if len(h) > k:
        heapq.heappop(h)                 # evict the smallest of the k+1
# h holds the k largest; h[0] is the k-th largest
