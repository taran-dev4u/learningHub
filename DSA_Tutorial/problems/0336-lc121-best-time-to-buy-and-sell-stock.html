📋 The problem

Given daily stock prices, choose one day to buy and a later day to sell to maximize profit (0 if no profit possible).

Restated in our own words — the LeetCode icon above opens the formal statement with exact constraints and examples.

🧭 How to think about it

You are given an array prices where prices[i] is the price of a given stock on the i-th day. You want to maximize your profit by choosing a single day to buy one stock and choosing a different day in the future to sell that stock. Return the maximum profit you can achieve.

🐢 Brute force vs optimal

Testing all pairs of buy/sell days takes O(N^2). Greedily tracking the lowest price seen so far allows computing maximum profit in a single linear pass in O(N) time and O(1) space.

💡 Key insight: If you sell on day i, your optimal buy day is simply the day with the minimum stock price among days 0 through i - 1! Maintain min_price and update max_profit = max(max_profit, price - min_price).

🪜 The approach, step by step

  1. Initialize min_price = float('inf') and max_profit = 0.
  2. For each price in prices:
    • Update min_price = min(min_price, price).
    • Update max_profit = max(max_profit, price - min_price).
  3. Return max_profit.

✅ The solution

Reveal the commented solution (attempt it first!) 
def maxProfit(prices: list[int]) -> int:
    """
    Finds maximum single-transaction stock profit in O(N) time and O(1) space.
    """
    min_price = float('inf')
    max_profit = 0
    
    for price in prices:
        if price < min_price:
            min_price = price
        elif price - min_price > max_profit:
            max_profit = price - min_price
            
    return max_profit

⚡ Complexity analysis

  • Time Complexity: O(N).
  • Space Complexity: O(1).
