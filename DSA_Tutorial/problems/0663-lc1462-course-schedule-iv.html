Where this fits

Topological-Sort Applications — Topological-sort applications: scheduling, alien dictionary, sequence reconstruction, course graphs.

Why this pattern?

  • "cheapest / fastest path" with weights → Dijkstra (non-negative)
  • negative weights or "at most k stops" → Bellman-Ford / k-round relaxation
  • "minimum cost to connect all" → MST (Kruskal + DSU, or Prim)
  • "critical connection" whose removal disconnects → bridges (Tarjan)
