📋 The problem

Design a class that accepts streaming numbers and always reports the k-th largest so far.

Restated in our own words — the LeetCode icon above opens the formal statement with exact constraints and examples.

Where this fits

Top K Elements / Selection / Frequency — Min-heap of size k to find the k largest, or max-heap of size k for k smallest.

💡 Key idea
Keep a min-heap of the k largest seen; its root IS the k-th largest at all times.

Why this pattern?

  • "k largest / k closest / k most frequent / k-th …"
  • merge k sorted lists/streams
  • "median of a stream" / balance two halves → two heaps
  • scheduling: always take the earliest end / most frequent / cheapest next

Pattern template to adapt

import heapq
# Top-k largest: min-heap of size k
h = []
for x in nums:
    heapq.heappush(h, x)
    if len(h) > k:
        heapq.heappop(h)                 # evict the smallest of the k+1
# h holds the k largest; h[0] is the k-th largest
