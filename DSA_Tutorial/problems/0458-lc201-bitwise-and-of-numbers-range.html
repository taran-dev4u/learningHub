#201 Bitwise AND of Numbers Range MediumLeetCode

📋 The problem

Return the bitwise AND of all numbers in the range [left, right] inclusive.

Restated in our own words — the LeetCode icon above opens the formal statement with exact constraints and examples.

🧭 How to think about it

Given two integers left and right that represent the range [left, right], return the bitwise AND of all numbers in this range, inclusive.

If $left < right$, there is at least one even and one odd number in the range, which means the lowest bit (LSB) will alternate between 0 and 1, making the LSB of the cumulative AND guaranteed to be 0!

In fact, as numbers increment from $left$ to $right$, all bits after the common prefix will toggle between 0 and 1 at least once. Therefore, the bitwise AND of $[left, right]$ is simply the longest common binary prefix of $left$ and $right$, followed by trailing zeros!

🐢 Brute force vs optimal

Iterating $left \dots right$ is $O(right - left)$ and TLEs for $2^{31}$. Shifting right until $left == right$ takes at most 32 operations ($O(1)$ time).

💡 Key insight: Find the common binary prefix of left and right: while left < right: right &= (right - 1). Clears non-matching suffix bits instantly!

✅ The solution

Reveal the commented solution (attempt it first!) 
def rangeBitwiseAnd(left: int, right: int) -> int:
    while left < right:
        right &= (right - 1)
    return right

Complexity

Time: $O(1)$ — At most 32 bit-clearing operations.
Space: $O(1)$ — Modifies integer registers.
