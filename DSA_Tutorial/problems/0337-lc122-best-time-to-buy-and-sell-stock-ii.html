📋 The problem

With unlimited buy-sell transactions (sell before buying again), return the maximum total profit from a price series.

Restated in our own words — the LeetCode icon above opens the formal statement with exact constraints and examples.

🧭 How to think about it

You are given an integer array prices where prices[i] is the price of a given stock on the i-th day. On each day, you may decide to buy and/or sell the stock. You can only hold at most one share of the stock at any time. Find and return the maximum profit you can achieve.

🐢 Brute force vs optimal

Because you can make unlimited transactions, you can capture **every single price increase** from day to day! If prices are [1, 5, 8]: - Buying at 1 and selling at 8 gives profit: 8 - 1 = 7. - Capturing daily gains: (5 - 1) + (8 - 5) = 4 + 3 = 7! The sum of all upward slopes equals the total gain from valley to peak!

💡 Key insight: Whenever prices[i] > prices[i - 1], greedily add prices[i] - prices[i - 1] to profit! If tomorrow is higher than today, buy today and sell tomorrow.

✅ The solution

Reveal the commented solution (attempt it first!) 
def maxProfit(prices: list[int]) -> int:
    """
    Captures all positive price deltas across consecutive trading days.
    """
    profit = 0
    for i in range(1, len(prices)):
        if prices[i] > prices[i - 1]:
            profit += prices[i] - prices[i - 1]
    return profit

⚡ Complexity analysis

  • Time Complexity: O(N).
  • Space Complexity: O(1).
