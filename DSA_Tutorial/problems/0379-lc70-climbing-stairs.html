📋 The problem

You climb a staircase taking 1 or 2 steps at a time; count the distinct ways to reach step n.

Restated in our own words — the LeetCode icon above opens the formal statement with exact constraints and examples.

🧭 How to think about it

You are climbing a staircase. It takes n steps to reach the top. Each time you can either climb 1 or 2 steps. In how many distinct ways can you climb to the top?

To reach step i, you must take either a 1-step from step i - 1 or a 2-step from step i - 2. Therefore, the number of distinct ways to reach step i is the sum of ways to reach step i - 1 and step i - 2: $$dp[i] = dp[i - 1] + dp[i - 2]$$ This is the famous Fibonacci sequence with base cases dp[1] = 1 and dp[2] = 2.

🐢 Brute force vs optimal

Naive recursion calculates overlapping subproblems exponentially: O(2^N). Dynamic programming caches subproblems in O(N) time. Because each state only depends on the previous two values, we can reduce space to O(1) using two variables!

💡 Key insight: Maintain prev1 (ways to reach step i - 1) and prev2 (ways to reach step i - 2). At each step: curr = prev1 + prev2; prev2 = prev1; prev1 = curr. Runs in O(N) time with O(1) memory!

✅ The solution

def climbStairs(n: int) -> int:
    """
    Computes distinct ways to reach step n using O(1) space Fibonacci DP.
    """
    if n <= 2:
        return n
        
    prev2, prev1 = 1, 2
    for _ in range(3, n + 1):
        curr = prev1 + prev2
        prev2 = prev1
        prev1 = curr
        
    return prev1

⚡ Complexity analysis

  • Time Complexity: O(N). Single loop of N - 2 iterations.
  • Space Complexity: O(1) constant extra space.
